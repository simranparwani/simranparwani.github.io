Hi, I'm Simran (she/her).

I've produced data visualizations with a wide range of contexts, tools, and teams—from Axios to FiveThirtyEight to the Census Bureau. While I think of data viz as my craft, I want to go back to my educational roots in creative technology and be more expansive in the data experiences I create.

I love the intersection of data and community-building: teaching "Data Visualization and Communication" at the University of Vermont and volunteering with the Data Viz Society. I love yapping and reading about data—except during my daily coffee walk or during a football game when I'm screaming for (at?) the Bengals.
